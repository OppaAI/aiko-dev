<h3>🤖 × 🪰🧠 AI x Fruit Fly Brain Part 2</h3><p>🧠🤖 Aiko is now connected to an entire fruit-fly brain connectome.<br/>I’ve been experimenting with something a little unusual: taking Aiko, my local AI companion/agent, and connecting parts of her cognitive architecture to the <strong>MaleCNS v1.0 fruit-fly connectome</strong>.<br/>And the latest update is a big step forward.<br/>Aiko now has access to a <strong>166,606-neuron / 25.6-million-connection</strong> representation of the MaleCNS brain.</p><p>The result is a hybrid architecture:<br/>🪰 <strong>Mushroom Body</strong><br/>→ real connectome-derived neurons and synapses<br/>→ associative memory<br/>→ learned approach/avoidance<br/>→ dopamine/reinforcement-based plasticity<br/>→ persistent learned changes</p><p>🧭 <strong>Central Complex</strong><br/>→ real connectome-derived circuitry<br/>→ focus/heading<br/>→ decisiveness<br/>→ sleep pressure</p><p>👁️ <strong>Fly sensory circuits</strong><br/>→ antennal-lobe-inspired sensory normalization<br/>→ T4/T5 motion processing<br/>→ sensory gain and motion salience</p><p>🧠 <strong>Whole MaleCNS graph</strong><br/>→ 166,606 neurons<br/>→ 25.6M directed connections<br/>→ two-hop rate propagation<br/>→ descending-neuron, clock and arousal readouts</p><p>Then all of those signals feed into Aiko’s existing AI architecture through a shared <strong>NeuralState</strong>.<br/>That means the fly-derived circuitry can influence things such as:<br/>• what she remembers<br/>• what she approaches or avoids<br/>• where her attention goes<br/>• how urgent something feels<br/>• how energetic or tired she is<br/>• her circadian state<br/>• how strongly she speaks<br/>• her expression and body behavior<br/>• how cautiously she approaches certain external actions</p><p>So instead of:<br/><strong>LLM → response</strong><br/>the architecture is becoming more like:<br/><strong>senses → biological circuits → neural state → memory / reasoning / agent → voice / body</strong><br/>And now I can actually watch part of that process happening in the Fly Brain Neuralnet Studio.<br/>The visualization in this video is showing a sampled/procedural view of the much larger connectome, together with Aiko’s live neural-state and output signals.</p><p>There is still a lot left to do.<br/>The current whole-brain layer is rate-based rather than spiking, the dynamics are partly functional abstractions, and many biological circuits are still represented as scaffolds rather than complete biological simulations.</p><p>But this is the direction I wanted to explore:<br/><strong>What happens when an AI agent doesn’t just use an LLM, memory and tools, but also has a small piece of real biological network architecture influencing how it processes experience?</strong></p><p>🧠 × 🪰 × 🤖<br/>Github: <a href="https://github.com/OppaAI/Aiko-chan" target="_blank">https://github.com/OppaAI/Aiko-chan</a><br/>The MaleCNS connectome work is based on the open research datasets from Janelia/FlyEM and related open-source projects.</p><p><strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/all/?keywords=%23ai&amp;origin=HASH_TAG_FROM_FEED"
